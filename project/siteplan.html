Project: Eternally Yours — a wedding website

Site Name

Eternally Yours

The name was chosen because it captures the lasting, romantic promise at the heart of a wedding without tying the site to one specific couple's names — making it easy to reuse the template for different clients. It reads warmly in a classic, timeless voice, which matches the romantic/classic direction of the design.

Optional domain availability: eternally-yours.love

Site Purpose

The site gives engaged couples and their guests a single, elegant hub for everything related to the wedding day. It presents the couple's story, shares the ceremony and reception details, collects RSVPs, and points guests toward the gift registry — replacing scattered group chats and paper invitations with one graceful digital experience.

Scenarios

  • Where and at what time is the wedding ceremony and reception taking place?
  • How do I RSVP, and can I let the couple know if I'm bringing a guest or have dietary restrictions?
  • Where is the couple registered, in case I'd like to send a gift?
